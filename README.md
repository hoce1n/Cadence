# Cadence

Daily habits, kept.

Cadence is a quiet habit journal. It is for the few things you want to keep showing up for — not a feed, not a scoreboard, not another account.

Paper, ink, and a week of checks. That is the whole idea.

## What it does

Each habit has a name, a color, and a history of days you marked. Cadence turns that into:

- **Today** — how many habits are still open, the best live streak, and this week’s rate
- **Week** — a color-coded grid. Tap a square to keep the day, or tap again to undo
- **Month** — a heatmap of the whole month, a selected day’s checklist, and a strip per habit so streaks are easy to scan

Stats stay close to the habit they belong to: current streak, best streak, and how the last thirty days actually went.

## Habits

Add, rename, recolor, or delete. Twelve is the ceiling, so the page stays scannable instead of becoming a list you stop looking at.

Colors are muted on purpose: sage, teal, slate, clay, rose, olive, and ink. They code the calendar, not the chrome.

Future days stay closed. Past days can be corrected. A streak holds through this morning until you miss a full day — so opening the journal does not reset you to zero.

## Privacy

Everything lives on this device. There is no sign-in, no cloud copy, and no sharing. Clearing site data clears the journal.

## Design

Cadence is meant to feel like a page you return to. A serif wordmark, warm paper, and one restrained sage accent. The calendar is the product; the rest of the interface stays out of the way.
